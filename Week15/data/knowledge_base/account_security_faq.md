# ShopAssist AI - Account Management & Security FAQ

## 1. Creating and Updating an Account
- **Registration**: You can create an account using a valid email address or via Single Sign-On (Google, Apple, GitHub).
- **Profile Edits**: Visit **Settings > Profile** to update your name, contact phone number, default billing address, and email preferences.
- **Switching Accounts**: To switch accounts, log out via the navigation avatar and log back in with your secondary credentials. If you accidentally made a purchase under a guest session, contact support with your Order ID and target account email to link the order.

## 2. Password Reset & Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
- **Password Reset**: If you forget your password, click "Forgot Password" on the login page. A secure reset link valid for 15 minutes is sent to your registered email.
- **Two-Factor Authentication**: We strongly recommend enabling TOTP-based 2FA (Google Authenticator, Authy). Go to **Settings > Security > Enable 2FA**.
- **Account Lockout**: For your protection, five consecutive failed login attempts will temporarily lock your account for 30 minutes.

## 3. Account Deletion & GDPR Privacy Rights
- **Data Privacy**: Customers may request a full export of their personal data or complete account deletion under GDPR / CCPA regulations.
- **Deletion Rules**: You cannot delete an account with active pending orders, active warranties, or open return dispute cases.
- **Processing Time**: Once verified by email, account deletion is finalized within 14 calendar days, purging all identifying records except those legally mandated for tax and accounting audits.

## 4. Newsletter & Communication Preferences
- You can manage email subscriptions at any time via **Settings > Notifications**.
- Unsubscribing from marketing promotions takes effect within 24 hours. Transactional emails (order receipts, shipping notices, password resets) cannot be opted out of.
