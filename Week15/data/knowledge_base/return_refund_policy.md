# ShopAssist AI - Return & Refund Policy

## 1. General Return Window
Customers may initiate a return for eligible items within **30 calendar days** from the date of confirmed delivery. Returns initiated after 30 days are not accepted unless covered under an extended manufacturer warranty.

## 2. Item Condition Requirements
To be eligible for a full refund:
- Items must be in their original, unwashed, and undamaged condition.
- Original packaging, tags, barcodes, accessories, and user manuals must be intact.
- Items showing signs of customer misuse, accidental drops, or deliberate damage will be rejected upon inspection at our fulfillment warehouse.

## 3. Non-Returnable & Final Sale Categories
The following items cannot be returned due to health, hygiene, and copyright policies:
- Perishable goods, food items, and groceries.
- Intimate apparel, swimwear, and personal hygiene products once the hygiene seal is broken.
- Opened software licenses, gift cards, and downloadable digital products.
- Items marked as "Final Sale" or "Clearance" at checkout.

## 4. Refund Processing Timelines
Once our warehouse receives and inspects the returned item:
- **Quality Inspection**: 2 business days from receipt.
- **Refund Issuance**: Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method.
- **Bank Posting Time**:
  - Credit/Debit Cards: 3 to 5 business days.
  - PayPal / Digital Wallets: 1 to 2 business days.
  - Store Credit: Instant upon return approval.

## 5. Return Shipping Costs
- **Defective / Damaged / Incorrect Item**: ShopAssist AI provides a pre-paid return shipping label at zero cost.
- **Change of Mind / Buyer Preference**: A flat return shipping label fee of **$5.99** is deducted from the final refund amount.
