# ShopAssist AI - Shipping & Delivery Guide

## 1. Shipping Methods & Estimated Delivery Times
We partner with major logistics couriers (FedEx, UPS, DHL, and Postal Express) to provide reliable delivery services:
- **Standard Shipping**: 3 to 5 business days across continental regions. Free on all orders over $50. Flat $4.99 for orders under $50.
- **Express / Expedited Shipping**: 1 to 2 business days. Flat $12.99. Orders must be placed before 2:00 PM local time for same-day dispatch.
- **Overnight Priority Delivery**: Next business day delivery guaranteed by 10:30 AM or 3:00 PM depending on zip code. Flat $24.99.
- **International Shipping**: 7 to 14 business days depending on customs clearance. Rates vary by destination country and parcel weight.

## 2. Order Processing & Tracking
- All orders placed Monday through Friday before 3:00 PM EST enter warehouse picking and packing within 4 hours.
- A tracking number with a direct courier portal link is automatically dispatched via email and SMS once the shipping label is scanned.
- Tracking status updates may take up to 12 to 24 hours to appear on courier tracking pages after label generation.

## 3. Shipping Address Modifications
- **Within 2 hours of placement**: Customers may freely modify delivery addresses via the self-service portal or customer support agent.
- **After dispatch**: Once a tracking number is active and picked up by the courier, address changes require courier rerouting and incur a **$10.00** carrier reroute surcharge. Rerouting is subject to courier feasibility.

## 4. Lost, Stolen, or Damaged in Transit
- If tracking shows "Delivered" but the parcel is missing, allow a 24-hour buffer as couriers occasionally mark packages early during final route sorting.
- If the parcel remains missing after 24 hours, contact customer support within 7 days to file an official carrier trace and claim.
- Replacements or full refunds are expedited for verified transit loss or transit damages.
