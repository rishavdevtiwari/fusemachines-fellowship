# ShopAssist AI - Order Cancellation & Fee Structure

## 1. Cancellation Windows & Fee Schedule
Our order fulfillment center begins automated sorting immediately upon payment authorization. Cancellation fees depend on the elapsed time between order confirmation and cancellation request:

| Fulfillment Stage | Elapsed Time Since Order | Applicable Fee | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stage 1: Grace Period** | 0 to 1 hour | **$0.00 (100% Free)** | Instant automated cancellation and 100% refund. |
| **Stage 2: Processing & Staging** | 1 to 6 hours | **$5.00 flat fee** | Restocking and administrative fee for unpacked orders. |
| **Stage 3: Picking & Boxing** | 6 to 24 hours | **$15.00 flat fee** | Order palletized; requires manual warehouse recovery. |
| **Stage 4: Dispatched / In Transit** | > 24 hours or Shipped | **Cannot Cancel directly** | Must be received and returned under standard Return Policy ($5.99 return shipping deduction). |

## 2. Custom & Made-to-Order Items
- Personalized, engraved, or customized items cannot be canceled once fabrication begins (typically 2 hours after order submission).
- If canceled prior to production start, a **15% material restocking fee** applies.

## 3. How to Request Cancellation
- Online: Log into your account, visit **Orders**, select the pending order, and click **Request Cancellation**.
- AI Assistant / Support: Provide your Order ID (e.g., `ORD-10023`) to the support assistant. The assistant will check warehouse status, calculate applicable fees, and process cancellation immediately.

## 4. Refund of Remaining Balance
Upon successful cancellation, any net refund balance (Total Order Amount minus applicable cancellation fee) is initiated automatically within 1 hour and credited to the original funding source within 3-5 business days.
